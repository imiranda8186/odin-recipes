Pasta Recipe

Description

Creamy and fulfilling Fettucine Alfredo. Best served alone, or with toppings such as chicken, broccoli, or shrimp.

Ingredients List

  • 24 ounces dry fettucine pasta
  • 1 cup butter
  • 3/4 pint heavy cream
  • 1 pinch garlic salt
  • salt and ground black pepper
  • 3/4 cup grated Romano cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese

Recipe Steps

  1. Fill a large pot with salted water and bring to a boil. Cook fettucine at a boil until tender yet firm, about 8 minutes. Drain
  2. Heat butter and cream in a large saucepan over low heat until butter melts. Add garlic salt, salt, and black pepper.
  3. Increase heat to medium. Stir in both cheese until melted.
  4. Add cooked pasta to sauce. Toss until thoroughly coated and serve.
